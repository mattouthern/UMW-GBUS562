Debit Card Dispute Procedure - How much the member may have to pay
Version 4 (current). Owner: Card Services.

A member's share of the loss from unauthorized debit card transactions depends on how quickly they report it. If the member reports a lost or stolen card within two business days of learning about it, they pay no more than $50. If they report it later than that, they may pay up to $500. If unauthorized transactions appear on a statement and the member does not report them within 60 days after the statement was sent, they may have to pay for transactions after those 60 days in full.

Debit Card Dispute Procedure - When a member reports a transaction they did not make
Version 4 (current). Owner: Card Services.

When a member says a debit card transaction on their account was not made or authorized by them, freeze the card right away in the card system, then open a dispute case. Order a replacement card. The replacement is free when the card was used for fraud.

Debit Card Dispute Procedure - Provisional credit
Version 4 (current). Owner: Card Services.

Larkfield investigates every dispute. If the investigation is not finished within 10 business days, the member receives a provisional credit for the disputed amount while it continues. If the investigation finds the transaction was authorized, the provisional credit is reversed and the member is told in writing.

Debit Card Dispute Procedure - The dispute form
Version 4 (current). Owner: Card Services.

The member must sign a dispute form listing each transaction they are disputing. The form can be signed at a branch or electronically through online banking. The investigation begins when Larkfield receives the signed form.
